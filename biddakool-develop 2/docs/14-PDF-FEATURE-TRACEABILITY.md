## Detailed requirements coverage summary

- Student and staff: admissions, recruitment, profiles, ID design, attendance, leave, certificate issuance.
- Academics: flexible institutions, grading/result cards, annual weighting, tabulation, online results, merit lists, exam seating, routines, lesson plans, online quiz and LMS.
- Fees and finance: cash/online fees, receipts, dues, notices, vouchers, financial statements, ledger and payroll.
- Facility/other: inventory, library, hostel, donations, Hifz, messaging/SMS, admin committees and feedback.
- Portals: admin, teacher, guardian/student, with payroll, academic data, fee and notice views.
- Medical: clinical teaching attendance, low-attendance notification, multi-stage leave, item/card/term grades, professional exam eligibility and anonymous teacher evaluation.
- Appendix: multiple report card styles and tabulation variations to inform template system.
