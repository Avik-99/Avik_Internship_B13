# Project Charter & Milestones

## Vision

One secure, configurable educational institution operating platform for Bangladesh-first deployment, with institution-type-specific workflows and potential broader deployment without a forced schema rewrite.

## MVP acceptance (Milestone 1)

One tenant can configure program/class/term/course data; register students and faculty; record class/course attendance; conduct and publish exams; collect cash fees and issue receipts; make schedules and notices; and expose appropriate student, guardian, teacher and administrator views. Two tenants must be completely isolated.

## M2 acceptance

Public application/recruitment, advanced examination/reporting, online payments, accounting, payroll, leave/device attendance, messaging and teaching progress integrate without duplicating M1 records.

## M3 acceptance

Opt-in specialized services for inventory, library, hostel, donations, Hifz, LMS, medical exams/clinical teaching and enterprise SaaS; medical and regulatory policies are configurable and approved by the institution.

## Product constraints

- Institution type is a **configuration and policy dimension**, not separate incompatible apps.
- Academic year/term/curriculum and historical result data require version-safe domain models.
- Guardian access may not apply to all university students; obey consent and age/privacy policies.
- Finance and medical eligibility must have institutional policy sign-off.
- Brochure is **feature inspiration/reference**, not source code or an official regulatory standard.
