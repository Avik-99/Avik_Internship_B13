# Domain Glossary

**Tenant** — SaaS data and billing boundary. **Institution** — individual educational organization. **Campus** — physical/organizational site. **AcademicYear** — year grouping. **Term/Semester** — academic period. **Program** — educational pathway/qualification. **Course** — catalog subject or unit. **CourseOffering** — course delivered for a term/section/teacher. **ProgramEnrollment** — admitted to program. **CourseEnrollment** — registered in specific offering. **ClassPlacement** — school style class, section and roll assignment. **Assessment** — marks-bearing task/exam component. **ResultSnapshot** — immutable published calculation record. **FeeCharge** — student payable obligation. **PaymentAllocation** — amount applied to a specific due. **GuardianLink** — authorized relationship between person and student. **Hifz Group** — specialized madrasa study unit. **Professional Eligibility** — medical exam admissibility from approved attendance/mark policies. **Outbox/Inbox** — reliable event transmission/reception with deduplication. **ADR** — architecture decision record. **Tenant context** — validated current customer scope for a request/job.
